# Code of Conduct – Rahul Roy Portfolio

## 👋 Our Pledge

As the creator and maintainer of this portfolio, I, Rahul Roy, am committed to making this project a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive space for everyone.

Whether you're here to explore, collaborate, contribute, or learn — you are welcome.

---

## 🤝 Our Standards

To maintain a positive environment, I expect all interactions to follow these principles:

### ✅ Positive Behavior

* Be respectful and professional
* Encourage learning and growth
* Provide constructive feedback
* Respect different perspectives and experiences
* Support open collaboration

### ❌ Unacceptable Behavior

* Harassment, hate speech, or discrimination
* Trolling, insults, or personal attacks
* Sharing private information without permission
* Any behavior considered unprofessional or harmful

---

## 🛠 Responsibilities

As the project owner:

* I will review and manage contributions fairly
* I reserve the right to remove inappropriate content
* I aim to maintain a safe and professional environment

## 🌐 Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

* GitHub repository interactions

* Social media or public mentions related to this portfolio
